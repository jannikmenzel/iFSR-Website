## \[experiment] Aptitude Tests

Are you still unsure whether studying computer science is the right choice for you?\
Here you find aids to better assess your interests and prior knowledge.

> A comprehensive collection of aptitude tests is available [here](https://www.einstieg-informatik.de/?article_id=800%3Ftarget%3Dblank).

Don’t be discouraged by an unexpected test result!\
It can show you that you should reconsider your expectations about the subject.\
Conversely, a good result is of course no guarantee for an easy study—computer science remains challenging.

## \[newsstand] Preparatory Course Computer Science

Do you want to check how well you can handle the study content right away?\
Then try the **Preparatory Course Computer Science**!\
It covers the basics that play a role in the first semesters.

> Course info: [Preparatory Course Computer Science (OPAL)](https://bildungsportal.sachsen.de/opal/auth/RepositoryEntry/23217537051)

If you enjoy learning and keep up well, that’s a good sign for your study choice!
