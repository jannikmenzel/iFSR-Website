# Meetings & Protocols

The FSR meetings take place every **Monday at 6:30 pm** during the semester and usually every two weeks during the lecture-free period. Here we discuss current topics concerning the university and the Faculty of Computer Science. Extraordinary meetings will be announced in due time through the usual channels. The meeting takes place publicly in the [Council Chamber APB 1004](https://navigator.tu-dresden.de/etplan/apb/01/raum/542101.3070) and all students are warmly invited!

In addition to the in-person meeting, we also offer the possibility to participate online via BigBlueButton. All students can join via the following link:

> [To the online meeting (BBB)](https://ifsr.de/bbb)
