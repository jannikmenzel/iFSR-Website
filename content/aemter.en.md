# Offices

## \[campaign] Spokespersons

The spokespersons represent the Student Council externally and serve as contact persons for official matters. They are well connected, keep track of everything, and try to recognize problems early.

> "Of course, it's also part of the job to foster cohesion and a good atmosphere in the council. When conflicts arise, spokespersons should be able to mediate." – **Robert**

## \[paid] Treasurers

The treasurers are responsible for managing the Student Council's financial resources. They:
- pay invoices
- handle financial transactions properly
- ensure the account stays balanced
- prepare the annual financial statement at the end of the fiscal year

## \[folder_open] Structurers

They take care of internal operations and ensure the Student Council remains functional:
- procuring office supplies
- publishing meeting minutes
- forwarding committee assignments
- maintaining an overview of rules and structure
