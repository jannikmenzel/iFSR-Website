# Services

The iFSR offers a wide range of services. Whether you want to print or copy something, calculate your final grade, or borrow something from our selection of technology and board games — you’re in the right place.

---

## \[print] Printing & Copying

### Black & White

| Pages | Suggested Donation |
|-------|--------------------|
| 1–2   | 5 ct               |
| 3–5   | 10 ct              |
| 6–7   | 15 ct              |
| 8–10  | 20 ct              |

### Color

| Color Coverage | Suggested Donation | Example                           |
|----------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------|
| 100 %          | €1.20              | Full-page graphics or posters     |
| 75 %           | €0.90              | Large colorful diagrams           |
| 50 %           | €0.60              | Colorful headings & graphics      |
| 25 %           | €0.30              | e.g., lecture slides              |
| 10 %           | €0.10              | Some colored words, e.g., tickets |

> **More than 10 pages to print?** No — go to a copy shop for that!
