# Contact

The Computer Science Student Council is here to support you with all matters related to your studies. You can find our office in the APB building in [Room E017](https://navigator.tu-dresden.de/etplan/apb/00/raum/542100.2230), right next to [ascii](https://ascii-dresden.de/). Specific contact information for our study advising can be found [here](/content/en/study-advising.md) on our subpage. For quick questions that might also interest other students, feel free to write to us on our public Matrix channel. You can find the channel [here](https://matrix.to/#/#fsr-inf-studierende:matrix.org). The [Student Council (StuRa)](https://www.stura.tu-dresden.de/) or the [Student Services](https://www.studentenwerk-dresden.de/) are also possible contact points. An overview with contact details of your currently elected representatives can be found on the [FSR Members](/about/members) page.
